## Design and invariants

- PostgreSQL remains authoritative for event intent, per-consumer inbox completion, notification send state, retry budget, parking and replay leases. BullMQ acceptance/job state is transport evidence only. Delivery remains at-least-once; no exactly-once claim is made.
- Redis producer connections use `enableOfflineQueue: false`, `maxRetriesPerRequest: 1`, `connectTimeout: 5s`, and no reconnect retry strategy. The publisher applies a bounded enqueue deadline; an unknown enqueue result retains the durable event identity and is not called success.
- Workers use separate persistent ioredis connections, `maxRetriesPerRequest: null`, offline queueing and bounded jittered reconnect backoff (1s minimum, 30s maximum). Relay circuit delay is distinct from a consumer business attempt.
- The durable domain-event worker provider is registered only in the explicit test environment; outside `APP_ENV=test && NODE_ENV=test`, the provider resolves unavailable and readiness is degraded. Reconciliation/replay queue mutations are likewise guarded to test-only. This phase does not activate the production worker or relay.
- Consumer transient attempts are recorded durably in PostgreSQL, with five actual business attempts per replay cycle. Delivery retry backoff is exponential from 2s with 50% jitter, capped at 60s. Redis outage does not increment that counter. Definitive notification-not-sent behavior retains the existing three-attempt notification policy; uncertain outcomes are parked and never blindly resent.
- Terminal envelope/consumer/identity failures and ambiguous outcomes are durably parked by stable `(eventId, consumerKey)`. Parking upserts are idempotent and safe categories only; no payload, provider response, stack, PII or secret is stored.
- Reconciliation is bounded to 1–50 rows and uses a `(createdAt,eventId)` cursor. It consults PostgreSQL required-consumer coverage and completion receipts; missing Redis job state is not completion evidence. It reconstructs only eligible incomplete work with the original event/job ID. Failed queue work lacking durable failure evidence is parked as ambiguous rather than reset. Active replay leases and parked deliveries are retained.
- Replay is explicit, RBAC-protected (`domain_events.replay`), dry-run inspectable, audited, per consumer, lease-fenced and idempotent. No broad replay or identity regeneration. Sent/uncertain notification guards and inbox completion remain authoritative after queue loss.
- Shutdown stops claims, pauses/drains the worker, and enforces a 150s production deadline (with deterministic short test deadlines); a deadline returns `drained:false`, force-closes transport and does not mark work successful. PostgreSQL intent/receipt/notification leases remain recovery authority.
- `/health` is process liveness. `/ready` has a 750ms bounded check for database, producer and worker queue capabilities and returns 503 when a capability is unavailable. Authentication/security remains fail-closed.
