## Contract invariants
1. Event intent and consumer identity remain stable across retries, restart, duplicate legacy delivery and durable replay; consumer inbox key is eventId+consumerKey.
2. Notification intent plus its queued audit intent commit atomically; DB rollback leaves neither, and durable consumer completion is never acknowledged before all required writes.
3. Redis enqueue failure retains DB intent; recoverable state is not falsely described as queued at the transport layer or sent to a provider.
4. Concurrent recovery/retry workers cannot double-claim the same delivery; stale leases cannot acknowledge success.
5. Provider failure and uncertain/outcome-unknown must not be blindly resent; successes do not regress to pending or generate duplicate audit effects.
6. Retry budget distinguishes genuine provider/business attempts from Redis outages and transient infrastructure failures; exhausted effects are parked for authorized manual review.
7. Operator statuses disclose safe fields only: no recipient PII beyond contract, raw template content, provider payloads, secrets, authorization headers, raw customer documents or internal lease tokens.
8. Replay/reconciliation is bounded, RBAC-guarded, audited and scoped to eligible event/consumer identities; historical held rows remain immutable.
9. Failure of notification audit writes cannot leave a claimed sent status without persisted supporting evidence.
10. No real provider is called and normal runtime keeps its existing legacy_hold and cutover_pending fences. NO exactly-once transport claim.
11. No business effect or template is invented merely because a contract declares an event.
