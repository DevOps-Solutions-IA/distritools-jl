2. Quote totals, discounts, taxes and currency retain existing documented calculations; conflicting update/approval/convert races cannot double-execute effects or silently corrupt orders.
3. Any quote-to-order action is disabled/held when required external business policy or provider is absent; never fabricate a paid/settled shipment.
4. CRM customer relations are bounded, PII redacted without customers.read_sensitive, and no background writes on read-only requests. Customer export remains intentionally blocked.
5. Approved/published legal and footer content follows existing draft/approve/publish state machine; no unreviewed terms silently become public.
6. Footer/legal public serialization exposes only approved text and metadata; rejects credentials, internal notes, tokens, raw audit JSON, unsupported HTML/script content where existing contract disallows it.
7. Source mutation, audit and held outbox are atomic where an existing transaction contract requires it; injected audit/provider errors roll back or leave a safe recoverable intent.
8. Sensitive admin reads and mutations are audited without exposing secrets/PII; denied actions have no durable business side effect.
9. Bounded list/filter/export requests: strict query parsing, stable pagination and authorized CSV/PDF outputs, no formula injection or unbounded CPU/DB loads.
10. Preserve EX-04 event-bus routing/held/cutover fences; no provider activation, legacy replay, dangerous migrations or weakened tests.
