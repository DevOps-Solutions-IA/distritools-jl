## Required correctness invariants
1. An order and its items, totals, stock reservation, audit and domain-event intent are atomic under the documented UOW boundary.
2. Identical client idempotency keys return the same logical order/intent, including concurrent duplicate requests. Different payloads reusing identity must not silently succeed.
3. Order status transitions follow a documented source-of-truth graph and reject invalid, stale or reverse transitions.
4. Payment intent creation is idempotent and cannot fabricate successful capture/settlement.
5. Webhook validation rejects malformed, tampered, unauthenticated and unapproved signatures; verify raw-body requirements where they exist.
6. Repeated/out-of-order webhook events do not double-commit stock, duplicate payment/audit effects, or regress a terminal state.
7. Payment approval versus cancel, expiry and rejection are resolved atomically with deterministic locks and one winning terminal outcome.
8. Payment reversal/refund/chargeback semantics must not be guessed; evidence of unresolved business policy requires BLOCKED_DECISION while independent work continues.
9. Concurrent order create/checkout and stock reservations preserve nonnegative availability and single reservation reference.
10. Guest checkout rejects unsafe/unknown payment statuses; no PSE provider success is simulated as real.
11. Failed provider/unavailable network (test-only doubles) leaves recoverable, appropriately named intent; never reports PAID by assumption.
12. Public tracking/order/payment DTOs do not leak secrets, provider credentials, tokens, private internal audit/claim metadata or PII beyond existing contract.
13. Audit, inbox, outbox, notification intent and stock movement are consistent with transaction commit/rollback. No early success ACK.
14. Event-bus routing stays legacy_hold/cutover_pending per EX-04; no historical held replay and no missing business effects invented.
